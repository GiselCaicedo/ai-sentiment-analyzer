# Dataset source

The 80 English reviews in `sample_reviews.csv` were written for this course project. Bean & Leaf, its customers, and the events described are fictional. The data contains no customer records or personal information.

The sample covers eight café products over August–November 2026. Each row has a unique review ID, date, product, star rating, review text, and manually assigned sentiment. The reference labels include 37 positive, 20 neutral, and 23 negative reviews. October and sandwiches intentionally contain more complaints. Sarcasm, mixed feedback, and negation appear in several rows to illustrate difficult cases.

The hand-assigned label captures the overall intent of each review. The class balance and complaints are teaching examples. Model accuracy on this sample is descriptive of these 80 reviews.
